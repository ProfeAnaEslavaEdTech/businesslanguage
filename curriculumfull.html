Business English A1–C2

The curriculum in full: six levels, twelve units each, every unit specified — scenario, can-do, skills, target language, mission, success criteria, culture note and homework.

72 units216 lessons36 lessons per level50 minutesMediation from A2

How to read this

Each level is a 36-lesson programme: twelve units, three lessons each, delivered as three blocks of twelve classes. The three lessons of a unit are the same targets seen three times — input and practice, processing and rehearsal, then the mission — with the homework spaced +1 / +3 / +7 days across them.

Skills and resources are different things. The four CEFR skills — reading, listening, speaking, writing — are tagged on every unit, and mediation is marked where the unit is a relaying task. Grammar and vocabulary are resources: they are listed inside each unit because they feed production, not as skills of their own.
